# Social Inclusion, Human Development & AI Integration

Welcome to this repository. This space highlights my practical work in social assistance, engaging with street populations and vulnerable individuals, guiding them toward formal education, social reintegration, and digital literacy.

## 🌟 Core Pillars of the Methodology

* **Human Reconnection & Listening:** Utilizing over a decade of communication and public speaking skills to build trust, restore dignity, and actively listen to marginalized individuals.
* **Educational Bridge:** Guiding participants back to formal studies, basic literacy, and continuous learning programs as foundational steps for autonomy.
* **The Digital Horizon (AI & Modern Skills):** Introducing underserved communities to the realities of the digital economy, including basic data literacy and understanding how modern Artificial Intelligence tools shape the future of work.
